- **Outcome:**
    - We are Team Daimyoz.
    - We propose KAWAN, a system that goes beyond expectations as a companion for mental health.
    - KAWAN in Malay means friend. The primary goal of our project is to become a friend to the user in various aspects, from giving personalized advice to keeping journals, from developing soft skills to maintaining a healthy lifestyle, KAWAN is always with you.
